My Experience.

01

Bucknell University Admissions Ambassador

Summer 2026

Working as an Admissions Ambassador at Bucknell University has given me the opportunity to represent the university while connecting with prospective students and their families. During the summer of 2026, I helped lead campus tours, answer questions about student life, and share my own experiences as a Bucknell student.

One of my favorite parts of the role has been meeting students from different backgrounds and helping them imagine what their college experience could look like. As a first-generation college student myself, I understand how important it can be to have someone make the college process feel more approachable.

This experience has helped me strengthen my public speaking, communication, and interpersonal skills while giving me the opportunity to share my appreciation for Bucknell with others.

02

Milton Area High School Boys' Varsity Soccer Assistant Coach

Fall 2026

In the fall of 2026, I returned to my hometown to serve as a volunteer assistant coach for the Milton Area High School Boys' Varsity soccer program. Having graduated from Milton in 2025, the opportunity to return and contribute to a program in my own community has been especially meaningful.

As part of the coaching staff, I help with team and player statistics, support player development, and prepare the team for competition. My responsibilities also include working with players on technical and positional skills and contributing to coaching decisions during matches.

Beyond the competition itself, coaching has given me an opportunity to develop my leadership, communication, and decision-making skills while working with student-athletes. I've enjoyed being able to support their growth and give back to the school community that helped shape my own experiences.
