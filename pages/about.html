About Me

A Little About Me.

01

Where I'm From

I was born in Altoona, Pennsylvania, but my family relocated to Milton, Pennsylvania, when I was young. I grew up in Milton and graduated from Milton Area High School in 2025.

Growing up in a small, close-knit community has played an important role in shaping who I am today. My family has always been an important part of my life, and I'm grateful for the support and encouragement they've given me throughout the years. Through school, sports, and other activities, I've also been fortunate to build relationships with people who have supported me and encouraged me to pursue new opportunities. Those experiences have helped me appreciate the importance of connecting with others and staying involved in my community.

02

My Journey to Bucknell

Growing up in Milton, Pennsylvania, I lived just a short drive from Bucknell University. Although Bucknell was always close to home, I never imagined all the opportunities and experiences that would come from eventually attending a local university.

After graduating from high school, I began attending Bucknell University as a first-generation college student and a Gamgort Family Gateway Scholar.

My time at Bucknell has given me the opportunity to meet people from different backgrounds, explore new interests, and learn more about the possibilities available to me. One of the things I've enjoyed most has been building connections with professors, classmates, alumni, and professionals who have been willing to share their experiences and advice.

These opportunities have helped me grow both personally and professionally while encouraging me to continue exploring what I might want to pursue in the future.

03

Why I Chose Markets, Innovation & Design

My interest in marketing began during my sophomore year at Milton Area High School, when I took a Sports and Entertainment Marketing class. It was my first real introduction to marketing, and I remember how naturally the subject came to me. Something about it just clicked, and I knew it was an area I wanted to continue exploring.

During my junior and senior years, I participated in a two-year Career and Technical Education (CTE) program focused on entrepreneurship. Through the program, I took classes in accounting, personal finance, law, management, social media marketing, and entrepreneurship. These courses helped me develop a broader understanding of business while strengthening my interest in marketing.

When I began looking at colleges, I knew I wanted to find a strong marketing program. That's when I discovered Bucknell's Markets, Innovation & Design (MIDE) major. I was especially drawn to the program's emphasis on creativity and innovation, which stood out to me compared with a more traditional marketing degree. That approach was one of the main reasons I decided to apply to Bucknell.

04

Beyond the Classroom

Outside of academics, I've always enjoyed being involved in activities that allow me to meet new people and explore different interests. Sports have been an important part of my life, and I also have a strong interest in education and entrepreneurship.

At Bucknell, I've had the opportunity to work as an Admissions Ambassador, where I've enjoyed meeting prospective students and their families while sharing my own experiences at the university.

Giving back to my hometown community is also something that is important to me. I've had the opportunity to return to Milton Area High School as a volunteer assistant coach for the boys' varsity soccer team. I also established the Panther Promise Scholarship to support graduating seniors who are first-generation college students or have financial need. Both have given me ways to stay connected to the community that has supported me throughout my life.

I've also started exploring entrepreneurship through Oddfellows, a business-learning app project I'm developing. Working on this project has given me an opportunity to combine my interests in business, education, and technology while learning more about what goes into turning an idea into something real.

05

Looking Ahead

As I continue my time at Bucknell, I'm interested in finding opportunities that allow me to take what I'm learning in the classroom and apply it to real-world experiences. I'm especially interested in exploring careers in marketing and brand management, while continuing to develop my interests in entrepreneurship, education, and sports.

One of my biggest goals moving forward is to gain experience through internships, independent projects, and opportunities to work alongside people who have experience in these fields. While I'm not entirely sure where my career will take me yet, I'm looking forward to continuing to explore my interests and finding where I can make the greatest impact.
