01

Oddfellows

Oddfellows is an interactive business education platform that I'm developing to make learning about business more approachable, engaging, and accessible.

Inspired by the five majors within Bucknell University's Freeman College of Management, Oddfellows allows users to explore Accounting, Business Analytics, Finance, Management, and Marketing through interactive lessons, personalized learning pathways, and five original characters. My goal is to eventually create something that can benefit not only business students, but also individuals who haven't pursued a business degree or attended college.

With the help of AI-assisted development tools, I've been able to turn an idea into a working prototype featuring 50 interactive lessons, achievements, and character-based progression. Working on Oddfellows has been a great opportunity to explore entrepreneurship while learning more about what goes into developing a product from the ground up.

02

The Panther Promise Scholarship

The Panther Promise Scholarship is an initiative I established to give back to Milton Area High School and support graduating seniors who are first-generation college students or demonstrate financial need.

As someone who grew up in Milton and had the opportunity to pursue higher education with the support of scholarships, I wanted to find a way to help other students from my hometown pursue their own goals. I know firsthand how meaningful financial support can be when preparing for college, and I wanted to create something that could make a difference for another student.

I had the opportunity to introduce the scholarship at Milton Area High School's Senior Awards ceremony. Establishing the Panther Promise has been a meaningful way for me to stay connected to the community that helped shape who I am today, while supporting students as they prepare for their next chapter.
